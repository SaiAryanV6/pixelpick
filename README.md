# pixelpick

My app helps a gaming store decide which games to recommend for customers by comparing prices of games, playtime average, and ratings of players to find games that offer the best quality for their money.

> A course project for **MIST 4600: Computer Programming in Business** at the University of Georgia's Terry College of Business.

## Problem
Stores want to provide gaming recommendations that fall within their customers' budgets, but it's very hard to find this information when comparing prices, the amount of time a gamer spends playing a particular game (playtime), and how well other gamers rate a game. The purpose of PixelPick is to help stores see which games make the best use of their money.

## What it does
My app answers three questions:
1. **Budget:** Which highly rated games fit within a customer's budget?
2. **Value:** Which games offer the most hours of gameplay for their price?
3. **Best overall:** Which games have the best combination of affordable prices, high player ratings, and long average playtime?

## Data
The [Steam Games Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/artermiloff/steam-games-dataset) on Kaggle, collected in March 2025. The project uses a sample of 1,000 games.
